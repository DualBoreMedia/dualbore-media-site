# Shipment promise and delay handling: sales activation blocked

The owner approved **physical carrier handoff within 5 business days after payment confirmation**. Weekends and holidays do not count. Transit is additional. A printed label, queued email or tracking number is not shipment. Public support is [midohiomx@gmail.com](mailto:midohiomx@gmail.com), handled manually. This change activates no checkout, messages, signup form or scheduled job.

## Reasonable basis before accepting payment

The FTC requires a reasonable basis for the advertised time, including supply, expected demand and fulfillment capability. A database setting is not that evidence. The owner must document blank availability by design/size, decoration and packing capacity, inspection/rework allowance, staffing backup and carrier acceptance arrangements. Rehearse an actual made-to-order batch through handoff. Limit or pause order intake when committed work exceeds demonstrated capacity. No such capacity rehearsal has been verified in this candidate. [FTC business guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/business-guide-ftcs-mail-internet-or-telephone-order-merchandise-rule).

Approve the operating time zone, business weekdays, holiday dates and review horizon in `SHIPPING_BUSINESS_CALENDAR`. The schema is `{timeZone, weekdays, holidays, reviewedThrough}`; weekday numbers are JavaScript Sunday=0 through Saturday=6. Weekend and holiday exclusions are owner-approved; no final holiday list, operating calendar or cutoff is supplied. Confirm customer-facing calendar wording before sales. `SHIPPING_CALENDAR_APPROVED` stays false until that decision. The implementation counts qualifying dates after the payment date without assuming an order cutoff. A reviewed calendar snapshot freezes the original promise per order.

Payment webhooks use the signed event's original creation time, not its later arrival at our server. An invalid/missing timestamp preserves the earlier order receipt time and flags the promise unverified. Production handoff is blocked until that uncertainty is resolved. A future provider reconciliation must verify the original payment event; never edit the clock just to make an overdue order appear timely.

Admin and maintenance reconciliation retrieve the original paid Checkout event through Stripe's authenticated Events API, matching the session, order, mode and timestamp and following bounded pagination. Admin reconciliation can repair an earlier unverified timestamp after checking the original payment reference, currency and amount, with an audit record and no repeated inventory sale. Missing, expired or ambiguous evidence fails closed for manual merchant review; no timestamp is invented. Stripe documents only 30 days of Events history, so older cases require a separately verified support/refund resolution. This path has provider fixtures, not new live-provider validation. [Stripe Events API](https://docs.stripe.com/api/events/list).

## Daily work and exception control

1. Before opening sales, designate an accountable administrator and backup, and establish monitored reviews before every handoff deadline. The dashboard's Needs Attention shows due, missing-calendar, pending-consent, overdue and refund-required records. It is **not** an unattended deadline worker, email alert or guarantee that someone saw a task. Verify the staffed review process and an outage/backup procedure before setting `SHIPPING_DELAY_WORKFLOW_VERIFIED`.
2. Start production only on verified paid orders. Review supply and backlog continuously. When a miss becomes apparent, act promptly; do not wait until the deadline or assume a 30-day grace period.
3. An MFA-authorized administrator prepares the complete delay notice, with the actual reason, a revised handoff date supported by supply/capacity evidence, a response deadline no later than the current promise, a cancel/refund choice and the continuing right to cancel delayed unshipped merchandise. Copy the complete notice into a manual support reply. **This screen sends nothing.** Record the real sent time and message reference only after sending.
4. Record the actual customer's express acceptance or cancellation and its message reference. Silence never extends the promise in our stricter workflow. No prechecked consent, staff approval on the customer's behalf or retroactive notice. The order is held while awaiting consent. A second delay requires a new notice and new affirmative consent; prior notices/replies are retained.
5. If the customer cancels, or the consent/promise deadline passes, use the cancellation review action promptly. It blocks unshipped fulfillment and flags a required refund. An entirely unshipped order requires the full remaining payment including shipping and tax. A split shipment needs a reviewed allocation and applicable shipping/tax treatment; the application intentionally does not guess that amount. Have the administrator resolve it promptly and retain the basis.
6. Execute and verify the refund through the original payment method. A task, cancelled order or refund API request is not completed payment reversal. Retain the provider confirmation, amount, timestamp, audit actor and customer communication. Reconcile pending/failed/uncertain refunds without changing idempotency keys. Refund capability must remain available when new sales are paused: `REFUNDS_ENABLED` is a separate, default-false production switch, not controlled by the new-payment switch. Existing genuine refund duties must be handled directly with the merchant provider if website refund processing is unavailable.
7. Only confirm shipment after actual carrier possession. The staff/admin confirmation records authenticated actor and handoff time. Do not use it when merely creating a label. Capture acceptance evidence separately. Delivery is another event and must never be inferred from a label or promised processing period.

The governing rule defines shipment as carrier possession and specifies timely delay/cancellation options and prompt refunds; its usual third-party-card/other-method refund deadline is seven working days, with a different seller-credit rule. Resolve the applicable method and timing promptly rather than adding a made-up customer refund-processing guarantee. The implementation's affirmative-consent policy intentionally avoids relying on the rule's limited first-delay silence option. [16 CFR Part 435](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-435).
